⌖ CAMERA POSITION

Start centered behind the baseline, outside the run-off area, approximately 3–4 m high. Adjust height and setback to your lens and venue.

  • Include both baselines, sidelines, and player run-off.
  • Keep the net level and all four corners visible.
  • Avoid lights shining into the lens; test at match time.
  • Use a fixed wide shot. Digital zoom reduces useful detail.

FROM FIRST SERVE TO FINAL REPLAY

Your courtside guide.

Record → replay → share

  1. Connect the recording server and enable your browser camera, or select the configured NVR.
  2. Name your match, choose a court, and check the frame.
  3. Start recording. Mark moments and enter rally winners as you play.
  4. Stop & save. Keep a browser recording’s tab open until upload completes.
  5. Open Match library to watch, change speed, jump to moments or download.
  6. Create a share link valid for 14 days. Revoke it whenever needed.

Install as an app

On HTTPS, use your browser’s Install app option, or Safari’s Share → Add to Home Screen. The app shell works offline; server recordings, replay, and sharing need a connection.

Pickleball, built into the score

Traditional side-out scoring: only the serving team earns a point. Select the rally winner; the app handles server changes.

  • Doubles starts at 0–0–2. After the opening side-out, both partners get a service turn.
  • Singles changes service when the receiving player wins a rally.
  • Play to 11, 15 or 21, always win by two.
  • The return of serve and the next shot must each bounce before volleys begin.
  • No volley while touching the kitchen or its line, including momentum after the volley.

Score entry and moment markers are manual. The camera does not make line or fault calls.
